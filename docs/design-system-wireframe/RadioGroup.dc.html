Design System Wireframe RadioGroup · DS-15 · draft

RadioGroup

Pick exactly one of a list of options. Same row, box, label, focus, invalid and disabled treatment as Checkbox. The box stays square, like every shape in the system; selection is a black square dot inset in a white box, where a checked Checkbox fills black with a white tick. Group label, hint and error match CheckboxGroup. Built on Radix RadioGroup: one tab stop, arrow keys move and select.

Size sets the box, the dot and the row (hit area) height. The whole row, box plus label, is the click target. Same sizes as Checkbox.

02

Shape

Radio and Checkbox share the 2 px black square box. They differ in how "on" is drawn: Checkbox fills the box black with a white tick, Radio keeps the box white and insets a black square dot (half the box). Together with the role, the arrow-key behaviour and the group label, that is enough to tell them apart without a circle.

05

Item label

Same as Checkbox. The label always sits to the right of the box (left in RTL), in body text, not the uppercase Field label. Long labels wrap and the box aligns to the first line. An optional description sits under the label in caption gray-600.
