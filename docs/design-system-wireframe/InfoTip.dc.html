InfoTip

An info icon button that explains a field or setting in plain text. With a mouse or trackpad ((pointer: fine)) it is a Tooltip: dark, compact, opens on hover or keyboard focus. When touch is the primary input ((pointer: coarse)) it is a Popover: white panel with a black border, opens on tap. The switch reads only that media query, never the viewport width. Tooltip (text only) and Popover (any content) are also public components, built on Radix Tooltip and Radix Popover.

01

InfoTip: touch vs non-touch

Same trigger, same text, two behaviours. The trigger is a borderless 32 × 32 info icon button placed right after the label it explains. Its content is always non-interactive text: in Tooltip mode the content can never take focus, so links or buttons in it would be unreachable by keyboard.

02

InfoTip trigger: sizes and states

Borderless square icon button with the existing info icon in gray-600. md (32 × 32, 18 px icon) is the default, next to field labels and in help slots. sm (24 × 24, 16 px icon) sits inline with caption or label text. On coarse pointers the hit area grows to 44 × 44 with an invisible pseudo-element; the visible size and the layout do not change. Hover styles only apply under (hover: hover), so a tap never leaves a stuck hover fill.

03

Tooltip

Short plain-text label or hint for any focusable trigger, here an IconButton. gray-900 fill, white caption 13/20, padding 4 / 8, max width 240 px, square corners, no shadow. The 12 × 6 arrow is the same gray-900 and points at the trigger centre. The tip edge sits 4 px from the trigger (side offset), plus the arrow.

04

Popover

Non-modal panel opened by clicking a button; holds any content, including links, fields and buttons. Same look as the Dialog panel: white, 1 px black border, square corners, no shadow, padding 16. The 16 × 8 arrow is white with black edges and overlaps the panel border by 1 px, so the border opens where the arrow joins. Width 240–320 px from content, max 320.

05

Portal and layering

Tooltip and Popover content is portaled to document.body, so it escapes overflow clipping and stacking contexts. Fixed layer order: page, then Dialog overlay and panel, then Popover, then Tooltip. A Popover opened inside a Dialog sits above it; a Tooltip is always on top.

06

Delays and motion

Tooltip opens after 400 ms of hover, at once on keyboard focus. After one tip closes, the next one opens at once if the pointer reaches its trigger within 300 ms (scanning a toolbar). Popover has no delay. Both fade in and out over 120 ms (--ds-motion), no slide or scale, none under prefers-reduced-motion.
