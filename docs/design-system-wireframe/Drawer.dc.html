Design System Wireframe Drawer · DS-44 · draft

Drawer

Modal side panel that slides in from the right (default), left, top or bottom over the same 50 % black overlay as Dialog. Square corners, no shadow: a 1 px black border on the inner edge separates it from the page. Header, body and footer are Dialog's parts with Dialog's spacing; only the body scrolls. Under 480 px every drawer fills the screen. Built on Radix Dialog.

01

Side · right (default)

Size sets the panel's extent along the slide axis: width for left and right, height for top and bottom. sm 320 px, md 480 px, lg 640 px. The panel is pinned to its edge and spans the full cross axis. It never takes more than the viewport minus 48 px, so a strip of overlay stays visible to click. Stages below are drawn at 1:1.

07

Motion

The panel slides from its edge (translate 100 % of its own size to 0) while the overlay fades 0 → 1, both over --ds-motion (120 ms, cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)). Closing plays the same in reverse. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce there is no slide and no fade. Schematics, not to scale: dashed outline = resting position, overlay shown mid-fade.
