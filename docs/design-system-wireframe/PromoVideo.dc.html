Design System Wireframe Promo video storyboard · DS-74 · draft

Promo video

Storyboard for a 78 s, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps video (2340 frames) built in video/ with Remotion (DS-75). Message: don't spend tokens building your own design system. Eleven scenes; each artboard is one keyframe at the frame number in its header, in every theme that scene uses. Every keyframe is a 960 × 540 logical stage rendered at 2×, styled only through var(--ds-*) roles, and sets data-theme on its root; the token block carries tokens.css and themes/bold.css. Copy, frames, sources and motion: docs/specs/PromoVideo.md.

Motion vocabulary · frame-driven only

Five moves, all computed from useCurrentFrame() with Remotion interpolate (clamped) and the system curve from --ds-motion (cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1), passed as Easing.bezier). No CSS animation or transition (the DS-73 MotionOff stays mounted), no timers. Nothing zooms, spins or parallaxes.

09

Accessibility · contrast pairs per theme

Copy only uses pairs that checkContrast() requires at 4.5:1 (text roles on background, surface, fill and the status grounds; primary-contrast on primary; text-inverse on inverse). No copy sits on surface-subtle, which is not a checked ground. Focus ring and icons use the 3:1 UI pairs. Every chip below is drawn from the role itself in that theme.

Minimum hold per line of copy: max(60, 15 + 10 × words) frames fully visible after its entrance. Flashes: at most one light/dark reversal in any 75 frames (limit is 3 per second). No audio; a WebVTT text track and a described transcript ship with the video (spec, Accessibility).
