Design System Wireframe Toast · DS-39 · draft

Toast

Short, non-modal message that appears in a fixed region, then leaves by itself. Info, success and error. Each has a title, an optional description, an optional action (Undo, Retry) and a close button. Square corners and no shadow. A toast is separated from the page by its border. Severity shows in the fill (none or tinted), the border weight (1 or 2 px), the icon shape (circle, tick, triangle) and the title weight. Hue is never the only cue. Built on Radix Toast.

01

Severities

One size. 384 px wide on desktop and full width minus 16 px gutters on mobile (section 06). Every severity is shown without and with an action. The action is the existing secondary Button and sits under the text, aligned with the title. The close button is always present.

05

Stack

Up to 3 toasts are visible, in a plain column with 8 px gaps and no overlap. The system has no shadows, so cards stacked on top of each other would have nothing to separate them. The newest toast is nearest the screen edge, at the bottom. A fourth toast closes the oldest one.

07

Motion

There is one axis. Toasts come in from the right and leave to the right, the same direction as the swipe. Every animation is a CSS keyframe of --ds-motion (120 ms, cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)). They are keyframes and not transitions because Radix Presence waits for animationend before it unmounts. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every animation is removed.

08

Keyboard and F8 region

The viewport is a landmark region named "Notifications (F8)". Toasts never take focus on their own. F8 moves focus to the region from anywhere. Tab then starts at the newest toast (Radix). Focus anywhere in the region pauses every timer. On macOS the key may be fn + F8.

Timers pause while the pointer is over the viewport, while focus is inside it, and while the window is blurred. On resume they continue with the time left; they do not restart. A per-toast duration may lengthen these values, but never set it below 5000 ms.

10

Severity without hue

The same three toasts with filter: grayscale(1). The severities stay distinct with no colour: info has no fill and a hairline border, success has a tinted fill and a tick, and error has a 2 px border, a triangle and a bold title. Screen readers also hear a hidden "Success: " / "Error: " prefix.
